Cockpit shell v2 Approval mockup. Everything below is the app: Simple and Advanced, the tabs, Negative, Generate and the drawer all work.

What this page commits to

  • One light. Violet is on exactly five things: the iris, the active rail edge, the active tab underline, Generate, and the focus ring. Nothing else is tinted.
  • Metal, not cards. Platinum hairlines divide the inspector and the status bar. Two radii by role: 6 px on controls, 10 px on the canvas plate. Only Generate rounds to 8.
  • The eye is the progress indicator. The inner ring of the shipped sigil closes as the sampler runs, then the plate resolves. Painted widget in Qt, no new asset.
  • No backdrop blur anywhere. Panels are a solid fill with a 1 px platinum top highlight, which is the approximation the token spec already prescribes.
  • Type is IBM Plex Sans with tabular figures for every number. IBM Plex Serif italic appears twice: the wordmark and the empty-canvas line. Register both through QFontDatabase.
  • Simple hides rows in place. Switch to Advanced and watch the Sampling tab: the rows appear under the summary, nothing relocates.
